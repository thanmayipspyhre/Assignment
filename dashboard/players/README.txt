Player photos
=============
Drop one photo per player in this folder, named after the player in lower case with hyphens:

  suryakumar-yadav.jpg
  jasprit-bumrah.jpg
  ... (.jpg, .png or .webp all work)

Square or portrait photos work best (the page crops to a square, face centred at the top).
Any player without a photo shows their initials instead, so you can add them one by one.
Make sure you have the right to publish each photo.
